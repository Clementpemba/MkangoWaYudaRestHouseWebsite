About Us

Mkango wa Yuda Resthouse is a peaceful retreat on Likoma Island, surrounded by the crystal-clear waters of Lake Malawi.

We offer comfortable accommodation with stunning lake views, fresh local cuisine, and the warm hospitality that Malawi is famous for.

Whether you're here for relaxation, fishing, or exploring the island, we make sure your stay is unforgettable.

Perfect for couples, families, and solo travelers seeking peace and nature.
